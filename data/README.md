# Data documentation

The repository contains anonymized transcript CSV files for the train, validation, and test partitions. The original audio recordings are not publicly hosted in this repository.

The corpus comprises 7,380 utterances from 78 speakers (approximately 13.54 hours of speech), split by speaker:

- Train: 6,598 utterances, 70 speakers, 12.04 hours
- Validation: 392 utterances, 4 speakers, 0.76 hours
- Test: 390 utterances, 4 speakers, 0.73 hours

Participants provided written informed consent before recording and consented to public sharing of anonymized transcripts. Audio recordings remain subject to controlled access and applicable institutional conditions. Do not attempt to identify participants or redistribute restricted recordings.
